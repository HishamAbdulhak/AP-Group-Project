1. Introduction

University libraries and campus facilities play a crucial role in supporting teaching, learning, research, and student well-being. They provide access to academic resources, collaborative learning environments, and essential services that enhance the overall university experience.

This document outlines the key libraries and facilities available on campus, including their locations, services offered, and general usage guidelines. It is intended as a reference for students, academic staff, and visitors.

2. Overview of University Libraries

The university operates a network of libraries designed to support diverse academic disciplines and learning needs. These libraries provide both physical and digital resources, ensuring accessibility and convenience for all users.

Library Network Features:

Central main library serving the entire university

Faculty-specific and specialized libraries

Digital library services accessible remotely

Extended operating hours during examination periods
